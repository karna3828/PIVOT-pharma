      About the project

      Reading the Pivot is a pharmaceutical business intelligence prototype designed to compare management narratives with reported financial evidence. It helps researchers identify changes in business priorities, evaluate whether strategic claims are supported by disclosures, and monitor potential business-model shifts.

      1. Select a pharmaceutical company.
      2. Add annual reports and investor presentations.
      3. Record management narratives.
      4. Compare them with disclosed financial evidence.
      5. Identify potential strategic signals.
      6. Review the source evidence before drawing conclusions.
      “Don't just read what a company says. Examine what its evidence reveals.”
      Hackathon prototype. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. No company financials are included unless you enter them with a source.
